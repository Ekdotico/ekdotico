## What you may do without asking

- Say truthfully that your software is based on, works with, or is a fork of
  Ekdotico, in plain text and without the logo: "based on Ekdotico".
- Keep the name and the copyright notices in an unmodified copy of the source,
  as the licence requires.
- Use the name in plain text to refer to the project in articles, teaching,
  research and reviews.

## What needs written permission

- Using a name or mark as, or as part of, the name, logo or domain of another
  product, service, organisation or modified version.
- Offering a hosted service under the Ekdotico name, or in a way that suggests
  the holder runs or endorses it.
- Using the logo mark, or a confusingly similar mark, anywhere outside an
  unmodified copy of this repository.

## If you fork

Rename the fork and replace the Ekdotico name and marks wherever they appear
(the application's name, its pages and emails, and any logo) before you
distribute it or run it as a service.
