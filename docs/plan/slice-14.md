## 3. Design

**The two numbers** are named in one module, `src/lib/collab/presence.ts`, which loads in a browser as it is:

- `CONNECTIONS_PER_DOCUMENT = 10` and `COLLABORATOR_COLOURS = collaboratorSlots.length` (the package's own list, 10 in 0.29.0). The module throws when the first is larger than the second (FR-ED-17: "the connection limit is not raised above the number of colours in the set"), and a test fails when it is changed without the other.
- The limit is a constant and not an environment variable, because the **fixed statement of FR-ED-18 says "ten"**: a limit changed without the statement makes the statement false. `createCollabServer` takes the limit as an option that defaults to the constant, for the tests. Raising it is one edit here plus the words of the statement, which is Q31's decision. A test holds the statement and the constant together.

**Places** (`src/lib/collab/places.ts`, no I/O, a clock that can be given). A place is `{ documentId, slot, name }`. `reserve(documentId)` is **one synchronous step** that counts the document's places, refuses at the limit, and takes the lowest free slot: nothing is awaited between the count and the taking, so no number of concurrent handshakes passes ten (the library runs `onConnect` for each of them, and each awaits the authorisation first). A place is _pending_ until its connection exists (the `connected` hook), and _held_ after; it is released when the connection closes (a callback on the connection itself, the moment `close()` runs, and the `onDisconnect` hook as the second way), and a place that was never made into a connection is released where the load fails, and at last by its own expiry: a pending place older than 15 seconds does not count, the longest that an authorisation and a load can take with the database client's 5-second bounds. `release` takes the place and does nothing for one that has been released, so a late `onDisconnect` cannot free a slot that has been given to someone else.

**The record of a connection.** `recordConnection` and `CollabConnection` are unchanged (NFR-SE-03 (a)); the context of a connection is that record and `place`. The one test that holds the context exactly is changed to say so.

**What a connection may say of itself** (the carried-forward point of the review of slice 8): `beforeHandleAwareness`, with the pure function `pinAwareness` of `presence.ts`:

1. A connection speaks for one client identifier: the one it has already spoken with, or the first it uses if none of its own and none of the document's other connections holds it. Any other identifier in its update is dropped (a client cannot speak for another, or claim another's before it speaks). The identifiers a message speaks for are read **from the message's own bytes** (`awarenessClients`, in `beforeHandleMessage`) and not from the map the hook is given: the library decodes an update into a scratch `Awareness` that holds a state of its own, for a random identifier that is no one's, and it is the first entry of that map (found while building: taking the first entry took it).
2. The state that goes on is `{ user: { name, slot }, cursor? }`, rebuilt: `name` and `slot` are the server's (`place`), whatever the client sent; every other field is dropped; `cursor` is kept only if it is exactly two relative positions of the form Yjs writes, every number a whole number of at most 2^53 and every string at most 256 characters (a malformed one makes `createRelativePositionFromJSON` throw in **every peer's** editor, which is why it is checked here). A client that sends a bad cursor keeps its presence and loses its cursor.
3. A message of the type awareness longer than 4,096 bytes closes the connection, before it is decoded (`beforeHandleMessage`; a legitimate state is under 400).
4. The role does not matter here: a read-only connection's awareness is handled exactly as an editor's (the requirement: "each connected collaborator").

**The re-check** (`src/lib/collab/recheck.ts` for the decision, which is pure, and `server.ts` for the timer and the closing). Every 60 seconds, unless the last run has not ended. The period is the setting `COLLAB_RECHECK_MS`, 60000 where unset and **at most** that (FR-ED-41: "at least once every 60 seconds"; the settings refuse a longer one), so that a test of a role that changes does not wait a minute; the end-to-end tests set it to 3 seconds:

1. The open connections are those of `server.hocuspocus.documents`.
2. One query reads the rows of their sessions with the account's address. A session stands only where: the row exists and is the connection's user's; `expiresAt` is in the future (the 7 days of idleness); `createdAt` plus `sessionMaxSeconds` is in the future (the 30 days); the account is not a tombstone (its address is null). Not through the identity library: it reads the signed copy.
3. For each distinct pair of user and document, `authorise(userId, documentId, 'document.view')`: the one function every request asks. `not_found` and `forbidden` are the end of access.
4. `decide` returns, for each connection, `keep`, `close` with a reason, or `switch` with the record that `recordConnection` makes of the new role and permission set. A switch writes the new role and permissions into the connection's record, sets `connection.readOnly`, and, where the role or editability changed, sends the stateless message `{"kind":"role","role":…,"canEdit":…}`, which the client reads strictly (`readRoleNotice`).
5. Where something cannot be read (the database does not answer), the connections that reading was for are closed as `unavailable` (S14-3), as the notice of a deletion does where it cannot read the row: all of them where the sessions cannot be read, and the connections of one user and document where their access cannot. The client tries again and its handshake reads the row.

The notice period of a clearing (FR-ED-40 (i)) and the export lock (FR-ED-40 (f)) are in the re-check's text but have no column to read yet (`Document.clearingAt` arrives with the clearing); they are not checked, and the re-check says so at the place where they will be added.

**The client.** The provider's awareness is switched on (`DocumentConnection.awareness`); in the editor of a browser that can edit, a plugin of `@tiptap/y-tiptap` (`yCursorPlugin`, already a dependency) draws the others with builders of the application's own: a caret and a label, `aria-hidden`, with the colour of `user.slot` read from the package's tokens by a class for each slot; a slot outside 1 to the number of colours draws nothing. The client reads the role notice: the editor's `canEdit` follows it, the extensions are remade by the key that already does so for a reader, and the statement of section 6 is shown. Where the notice makes the connection editable, the client asks the server for its state (`forceSync`), which makes it send what the server dropped while it could not apply it: the server applies a later change only once the earlier ones it depends on have arrived, so without this nothing written after the change of role would be applied.

## 4. The cases

| Case                                                                                                | What happens                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One to ten connections to a document, any roles and any accounts, one account's two tabs among them | Accepted, each with the lowest free slot                                                                                            |
| The eleventh, from any account that holds a role                                                    | Refused with `capacity` before any state is sent; the client shows the statement, not the error state, and asks again in 30 seconds |
| The eleventh from an account that holds no role                                                     | `no-access`, as for a document that does not exist                                                                                  |
| Twenty-seven attempts at once on an empty document                                                  | Exactly ten are accepted, with ten different slots                                                                                  |
| One closes (a tab, the socket, the re-check)                                                        | Its place is free at once; the next arrival takes the lowest free slot, not the next number                                         |
| The load of a document fails after the place was taken                                              | The place is released                                                                                                               |
| A client sends `user: { name: 'Someone else', slot: 7 }`                                            | Peers see the real name and the connection's slot                                                                                   |
| A client sends a state for the identifier of another client                                         | Dropped; the other's state is unchanged                                                                                             |
| A client sends a cursor that is not two relative positions                                          | The state is kept without the cursor; no peer's editor throws                                                                       |
| A client sends awareness over 4,096 bytes                                                           | The connection is closed                                                                                                            |
| A Viewer's connection                                                                               | Counts, holds a slot, is accepted with its pinned name; its browser sends no cursor and draws none (S14-1)                          |
| A session's row is deleted, expired, or older than 30 days; the account is a tombstone              | Within one period: the connection is closed `session-ended`, and its place is free                                                  |
| A collaborator's row is deleted, or the document is deleted by no event                             | Within one period: closed `access-ended`                                                                                            |
| An Editor becomes a Viewer; a Viewer becomes an Editor                                              | The connection stays open; read-only in place, and editable in place; the client is told, and states it                             |
| Nothing changed                                                                                     | Nothing is done to the connection                                                                                                   |
| The re-check meets a fault                                                                          | Closed `unavailable` (S14-3)                                                                                                        |
| The previous run has not ended                                                                      | The new one does not start                                                                                                          |

## 5. Tests (each fails when its rule is broken)

Unit:

- `tests/unit/places.test.ts`: the limit; the lowest free slot, and a freed slot given to the next; a place released twice frees nothing the second time; a pending place that expired does not count, and a held one never expires; one synchronous step (`reserve` called a hundred times in one tick admits ten).
- `tests/unit/presence.test.ts`: the limit is not above the colours, and each is 10 for 0.29.0; the statement of FR-ED-18 is what the requirement prints, and says the number the limit is; `pinAwareness` for each case of section 3, 1 and 2; `readCursor` for what a bad cursor can be; the display name.
- `tests/unit/recheck.test.ts`: `decide` for every cause and every order of causes; the two periods of FR-AU-84 to the last moment.
- `tests/unit/settings.test.ts`: `COLLAB_RECHECK_MS` is 60000 where unset, may be shorter, and is refused longer.
- `tests/unit/collab-client.test.ts`: a document at the limit shows the capacity state for as long as it lasts (never the error state) and asks again at 30 seconds and no sooner; the role notice is read, a notice that is not exactly one is ignored, and a notice that makes the connection editable asks the server for its state so that what a later change depends on is sent.
- `tests/unit/editor-presence.test.ts`: the caret and the label for a slot, in the editor's own harness: the label's text is the name as text and never markup; a slot outside the range, a state without a cursor, and the reader's editor draw nothing.

Integration, `tests/integration/presence.test.ts`, the real server in this process with the real provider (one file at a time): the cases above, including the concurrent attempts (27 across three accounts), the order (a refusal for no role is `no-access` at a full document), the re-check with its period shortened (the session row deleted; expired; older than 30 days; the tombstone; the collaborator removed; the document deleted by no event; a role changed both ways, the update of a downgraded connection discarded and of an upgraded one applied; the identity library's `getSession` never called by a run; a fault; two runs never overlapping), and the default period read from the timer that `createCollabServer` sets, and cleared by `stop`.

End to end, `tests/e2e/editor.spec.ts` (the file that runs the collaboration server, with its re-check at 3 seconds): two browsers, an Owner and an invited Editor, each sees the other's named cursor, with the other's name, in the colour of its slot (`--collab-1`, `--collab-2`, as the browser resolves the tokens), the selection in the wash of its slot, and the cursor goes when the other closes; ten connections, the first page and nine made straight to the server, and a second page that is the eleventh, which shows the fixed statement, neither the sync error state nor the restoring state, and gets in at 30 seconds from its refusal once a place is free (the client's timer, with the clock advanced by Playwright's clock); a session ended in another tab, which the re-check ends with `session-ended`, stated once, with no socket opened afterwards; an Editor whose role is changed in the database to Viewer and back, who is made read-only in place and told in the words of section 6, and editable in place again, with no socket opened by its page.

**Mutations, each tried once on purpose and restored** (named in the pull request): the count is not atomic (the check moved after an `await`); the limit is `<=` ten; the place is not released at close; the capacity check before the authorisation; the name taken from the client; the clientId not pinned; the cursor not checked; the session row not read (the cookie cache's `getSession` used); the role not re-read; the document's deletion not seen; the slot not the lowest free.

## 6. Texts, new, for approval (plan Q6)

Only the statement of a change of role, which FR-ED-40 (b) asks for ("states the new role") and gives no words for. It follows the wording of the statement in `src/lib/documents/access.ts` ("The role of this account on the document…"), and is a status, neither an error nor the sync indicator's error state:

| Where                                                        | Text                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The role of the open connection becomes one that cannot edit | "The role of this account on this document is now Viewer. The document is read-only in this tab." (the role's name as `ROLE_NAME` writes it: Viewer, Reviewer) |
| The role becomes Editor or Owner                             | "The role of this account on this document is now Editor. The document can be edited in this tab."                                                             |

The reasons `capacity` and `session-ended`, which the protocol reserved, are used as they stand. The statement of the limit, and the statements of an ended access and of an ended session, are the ones already built.

## 8. Not done, and said plainly

- Nothing counts in Valkey and nothing runs with two instances (D174, D168).
- A browser other than Chromium is not run; no screen reader; no phone width for the cursors.
- The re-check does not read the notice period of a clearing or the export lock (no column yet).
- A display name changed in the account's settings is shown from the next connection. No event carries it.
- The re-check asks the database once for the sessions and once for each pair of user and document, one after another: a few hundred connections cost a few hundred queries a minute, and a run that outlasts the minute makes the next one wait. A batched read replaces it when a measurement (P8) says so.
- The place of a connection whose socket died without a close is held until the library notices (its ping, 30 seconds): a client that reconnects at once can meet the limit, and meets it as `capacity` and tries again.
