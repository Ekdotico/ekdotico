## 2. Data model

One migration, `20261026010000_document_clearing`:

- `Document.clearingAt timestamp(3) NULL`. The day of the deletion, as a copy of `DocumentClearing.deleteSharedAt` for a shared document (A103), and, for a document that is not shared, the time of the confirmation (S23-2). Not `@updatedAt`; no write of the clearing names `updatedAt` (a clearing is not an edit).
- `DocumentClearing`: `id`, `userId` (restricting relation to `User`, as `AccountDeletion`), `scope` (`ClearingScope`: `ALL`, `DELETED_ONLY`), `documentIds`, `sharedDocumentIds`, `cancelledDocumentIds` (`uuid[]`), `requestedAt`, `deleteSharedAt`, `completedAt`, and `emailsClaimedAt` (S23-3). By hand: the shared and the cancelled identifiers are among the documents; `deleteSharedAt` is set exactly where there is a shared document, and is later than `requestedAt`; `completedAt` is not before `requestedAt`; an index on `(userId)` and a partial one on the clearings not complete.
- Notifications: three kinds in the existing table. `clearing_notice` has `{ documentId, clearingId }`; `clearing_cancelled` has `{ documentId }`; `clearing_deleted` has `{ title }` and nothing else, the one place a title is stored for a purpose of its own (FR-AU-91: "which is kept in the notification for that purpose alone").
- A document taken out of a clearing is added to `cancelledDocumentIds` whether the Owner cancelled it or its ownership passed (S23-4).

Personal data (the table of slice 22, section 2): a new place. `DocumentClearing` holds the account's identifier and document identifiers, no personal field, and stays with the tombstone. `Notification.payload` of `clearing_deleted` holds a title, for 30 days, and belongs to the recipient; if the recipient's account is deleted it is retained against the tombstone and shown to no one (FR-ED-42), which is the same retention as the other notifications of a tombstone (L5).

## 3. Design

**One transaction confirms.** In order: the password and the session asked again (`standing`, as in slice 22); the Owner's documents of the scope that are not in a clearing, locked `FOR UPDATE` in the order of their identifiers (so that two confirmations, or a confirmation and an offer, cannot wait for each other in a circle); the account's row `FOR SHARE` and the record of its deletion (`holdOwnableAccount`: of a clearing and a confirmed deletion, one waits for the other); the identifiers compared with the request's as sets; the collaborators read for those documents (a document with a row is shared); `DocumentClearing` written; `clearingAt` set; the pending invitations of every document revoked, with their notifications; one notification for each collaborator of each shared document. After the commit: the event for each document; then, after the response, the deletion of what is due, and the email.

**Why the documents are locked and not only counted.** An invitation accepted a moment before the confirmation would otherwise be missed (the document would be treated as not shared and deleted at once under a person who has just joined it); one accepted a moment after finds its invitation revoked. Every change to who holds a role already takes the document's row (`lockDocument`), so the confirmation sees the winner's work. A document that is soft-deleted is not found by `lockDocument`, which is right for the writes that use it: the confirmation locks by its own statement, which does not filter on `deletedAt`.

**The deletion is one function.** `deletePermanently` (slice 8) takes the condition under which it may claim the row and two hooks. The retention passes `deletedAt <= retained AND clearingAt IS NULL`. The clearing passes `ownerId = the account AND clearingAt IN (the clearing's days) AND clearingAt <= now`, and on claiming sets `deletedAt` (where the document is not deleted yet) and tells the collaboration servers, which ends every collaborator's access and closes every connection before the images go. The claim is the lock against everything else: `answerOffer` and the cancellation refuse a document that holds one, and the claim refuses one whose Owner or day has changed, so a transfer or a cancellation that commits first leaves the document, and one that comes second is refused. The hook of the final transaction reads the row `FOR UPDATE` under the claim, writes the three notices' worth of work (invitations ended, the offer withdrawn, the notices of the period replaced by the notice of the deletion for each collaborator), and only then deletes the row.

**The re-check and the handshake read one column.** `recordConnection` is given the document row: with `clearingAt` set it drops `document.content.edit` from the permissions. That is the only place the rule is written, and `isReadOnly`, the handshake, `decide`, `applySwitch` and the role notice all follow from it.

**The conditions of the writes.** Every write that must not happen in a period names it: `renameDocument` (`clearingAt: null`), `deleteDocument`, `restoreDocument`, `creating` (an invitation, under the row's lock), `reserveImage` (under the row's lock). A write that finds the condition false answers by reading the row again: 409 where the column is set, 404 where the document is gone, so that the answer to a race is the true one.

**Timers.** `clearing-deletion` (hourly and at start, a claim for each document) and `clearing-email` (the backstop of the hand-over). The documents of one clearing are deleted one after the other, and one that cannot be deleted does not hold the others back.

## 7. Departures from Data models (to be carried to the page)

- `Document.clearingAt` is also set, to the time of the confirmation, for a document that is not shared (S23-2).
- `DocumentClearing.emailsClaimedAt` (S23-3).
- `DocumentClearing.cancelledDocumentIds` also lists a document whose ownership passed (S23-4).
- `DocumentClearing.userId` is a relation to `User` that restricts, and the migration holds the lists together (the shared and the cancelled documents are among the confirmed ones; the day exists exactly where a shared document does and is after the confirmation).
- The three kinds of notification (`clearing_notice`, `clearing_cancelled`, `clearing_deleted`) and their payloads (plan, disagreement 3). The `clearing_deleted` payload holds a title, for 30 days, and is deleted by the timer of the notices of removals.
- API design: the cancellation asks for no password and no code (S23-5, plan disagreement 8); the request names `scope` as `all` or `deleted`; the routes are `POST` and `DELETE /api/account/documents/clear` and `DELETE /api/account/documents/clear/[documentId]`; a body is read to 256 KiB (S23-7). `GET /api/account/deletion` is not built.
- The `document-deleted` event the claim publishes is the existing one; `clearing-changed` is new (FR-ED-40 (i)).
- The role notice of the collaboration protocol carries `deletedOn` (an ISO time) while the document is in a clearing; a notice without it, to a tab that knew a day, says that the document will not be deleted. The record of a connection (NFR-SE-03 (a)) gains the day, `deletedOn`.
- The list of documents and the list of deleted documents (`GET /api/documents`, `GET /api/documents/deleted`) carry `clearingAt`, the day a clearing deletes the document or null, so that a list can say so and withdraw Rename, Delete and Restore; it is shown to the people who hold a role on the document, who are told the day anyway.
- The plan's disagreement 10 (the re-check omits the notice period) is closed: the record is built from the document's row, so the re-check reads it.
- Cancelling a document that is already gone answers 404, as every document that is not there does (and so as not to show that it existed); the API design page says 409. The page should say 404 (review of pull request 60). Cancelling for all after the day answers 409 `too_late`, as for one; with nothing waiting at all, 409 `nothing_waiting`.
- An offer of ownership is neither made nor accepted after the day of its document: 409 `too_late`, as a cancellation that comes too late (FR-AU-91 "during that period"). Declining stays open.
- **The Owner who confirms a clearing is never notified**, of the confirmation, of the day, or of the deletion: the notices of FR-ED-42 are for "each person who holds a role on" the document, and the Owner holds none in the table of roles. The Owner sees the screen.

## 11. S23-1 built: the former collaborators of a document deleted before a clearing may open it

The owner's answer to S23-1, 26 October 2026 (questions page p60-1, "view"): during the notice period of a clearing, the people who held a role on a document that was already deleted before the clearing (treated as shared under FR-AU-91) may open it, read-only, until the day of its deletion, and not only take a draft export; the "Open the document" link of their notice then works. The owner is to reword FR-ED-36 ("only the Owner can see it" during the retention) and FR-AU-91 to say so; this section and the code cite the answer as "owner's answer to S23-1, 26 Oct 2026".

**What was built, and nothing more.**

- `authorise` (`src/lib/documents/access.ts`) holds `document.view` for a role that is not the Owner's, on a deleted document, while `noticeRuns` (the day ahead, and the document not claimed by the timer): the one place the rule is written. The collaboration handshake, the re-check, the page, the document's summary and the link to an image of the document all ask `authorise` for `document.view`, so each follows; no other permission is granted by it (the export keeps its own line).
- Read-only: the handshake's record leaves out `document.content.edit` while `clearingAt` is set (`recordConnection`, slice 23), so a former Editor's connection is read-only from its handshake and what it writes is discarded; the page is opened read-only (`canEdit` false), told the day (`deletedOn`), with the statement below. No route that writes opens: every other permission finds a deleted document not found.
- The image link is issued for the images of that document alone (its route looks the image up by its identifier and the document), so the page can show them; the export reads the store itself.
- After the day, once the timer has claimed the document, or once the clearing is cancelled for it: the 404 of today. An open connection ends by the re-check (within its period), by the notice of the claim, or by the notice of the cancellation.
- The Owner's view is unchanged: a deleted document is no page to the Owner, who meets it in the list of deleted documents.
- The settings page of sharing (`/documents/[id]/sharing`) is not one of the pages of a deleted document: it answers 404 for one, and the page of the document does not offer the link to it. Nothing there could be done to a deleted document, and its controls would only be refused.
- The page of such a document says so in a statement of its own, in the pattern of FR-AU-91's (D252: "its words are owed to the owner's approval"): `CLEARING_DELETED_NOW` in `src/lib/clearing/texts.ts`, beside `CLEARING_NOW`, which stays the statement of a live document in a clearing (the page tells the editor which: `alreadyDeleted`). **Text for approval (proposed, not yet approved):** "This document was deleted by its Owner and will be deleted permanently on [date]. You can read it and take a draft export. It cannot be edited." ([date] as `CLEARING_NOW` writes it.)
- A deleted document is in the scope of a clearing only "still within its retention" (FR-AU-91): `countScope` and the locked statement of `confirmClearing` leave out a deleted document whose retention has ended and one the timer has claimed, for both scopes (the retention's rule, `retainedSince`, and not `authorise`). Without this a document past its retention but not yet deleted could be given a period of 7 days and read by its former collaborators.
- Left as it is, on purpose: `GET /api/documents/[id]/permissions` answers a former collaborator on such a document (it reads their own role's permissions, as for a live document), and an open connection shows the names of the other former collaborators who have the document open (the presence list of FR-ED-17, as for a live one).
