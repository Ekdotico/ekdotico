# Slice 18: the reminder and the clearing of addresses

Written on 20 October 2026 against Docs commit `f4ec614` (the SRS files read here are unchanged at `origin/main`, `e667736`), and the draft register at `docs-decisions@d2ad757` (rows to D205; A81, D60, D101, D176, D202 read). Row 18 of `alpha-0.1.0-plan.md`: "The one reminder 3 days before expiry; the clearing of an invited address after 30 days; the clearing of an admission invitation's address 30 days after it is revoked or expires".

## 2. Data model

No migration. `DocumentInvitation.reminderClaimedAt` exists since slice 13 (unused until now); `respondedAt` is when a document invitation was declined or revoked; `AdmissionInvitation.revokedAt` is when an admission invitation was revoked; `expiresAt` is when either expired. "Expired" stays derived: a row still stored `PENDING` past its expiry is written `EXPIRED` by the clearing (it is `EXPIRED` for every purpose long before).

## 3. The timers

| Timer (name in `TIMERS`)          | What one run does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Answers                                            |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `invitation-reminder`             | Reads the candidates: pending and ahead of its expiry, `expiresAt` within the reminder period of now, claimed for its email, not marked `reminderClaimedAt`, with an address. For each, in one transaction: one conditional `UPDATE … WHERE` the same (so two runs, or a revocation or an acceptance in between, find it claimed or no longer pending) marks it; the account that holds the address (verified) and holds no role on the document gets the notification, in the same transaction. After the commit, one email. A failure is one line naming the invitation; an email is never tried again. An invitation created after the moment of its reminder (a period set under 3 days) gets none (S18-3) | 0 (it deletes nothing, as `ownership-offer-email`) |
| `invitation-address-clearing`     | Sets `email` to null on every document invitation that was declined or revoked 30 days ago or more (`respondedAt`), or expired 30 days ago or more (`expiresAt`, state `EXPIRED`, or `PENDING` and written `EXPIRED` in the same statement); only where `email` is not null                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | The number of addresses cleared                    |
| `admission-address-clearing`      | The same for admission invitations: revoked 30 days ago or more (`revokedAt`), expired 30 days ago or more (`expiresAt`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | The number of addresses cleared                    |
| `invitation-notification-removal` | Deletes the notifications of kinds `document_invitation` and `document_invitation_reminder` whose invitation is not live: not pending-and-ahead-of-expiry, not accepted, or gone. The list already leaves these out                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | The number deleted                                 |

The clearing is at exactly the period, not before: an invitation that ended at T is cleared by a run at T + 30 days, and not by one a millisecond before. The reminder is sent by a run at `expiresAt` − 3 days, and not by one a millisecond before; and by none at or after `expiresAt`.

What a cleared row holds: its identifier, document, role, inviter, token hash, state, and times; no address. Everything that reads `email` already treats null as "no address": `resendInvitation` finds nothing to send again, the page of the invitation says it is not open (`judge`), the list of pending invitations shows only open ones, `listInvitations` of admission leaves the address out (FR-AU-89 (b)). A repeated admission request with the same request identifier after the clearing is answered as invalid (the row has no address to compare), and the command issues a fresh identifier each time.

## 4. Texts

Fixed texts used verbatim (FR-ED-48): subject "Your invitation to a document in Ekdotico expires soon"; text "[Name] invited you to a document in Ekdotico as [role]. The invitation expires on [date]. ## 5. Tests (each fails when its rule is broken)

Unit: the email (subject and text verbatim, the link, the date in UTC, no title, no token); the settings (`INVITATION_REMINDER_DAYS`, `DOCUMENT_INVITATION_ADDRESS_DAYS`: default, refuse 0); `timers.test.ts` with the four timers.

Integration, one file at a time, `tests/integration/invitation-clearing.test.ts`, all with injected clocks (`now` is a parameter of each function):

- Reminder: one at 3 days and none a millisecond before; none at or after the expiry; the email and the notification once, whatever the number of runs (sequential and two at once); a restart (a second run, a run in a new module instance of the same database) sends none; none for an invitation that is revoked, accepted, declined, or expired, nor for one sent to no one (no account, the Owner's address, a collaborator's address) or whose address then held no verified account; a sent-again invitation has its own and the revoked one none; the email holds no title (a title that is distinctive), no token, and the sink holds exactly one file for the invitation after two runs; the claim is made before the email (a send that fails leaves the row claimed, nothing is sent again, one line names the invitation and no address); the notification goes with a revocation, a decline, a resend, and the deletion of the document, and stays at an acceptance (as the invitation's own); a period under 3 days gives no reminder.
- The list: the reminder appears in `GET /api/notifications` with the role, the name of the inviter, the expiry, and no title; marking it read works.
- Clearing: at exactly 30 days and not a millisecond before, for declined, revoked, and expired (stored `EXPIRED`, and stored `PENDING` past 30 days after the expiry); never for a pending invitation, an open one, or an accepted one; the address is null in the row afterwards and in nothing else (the row is read whole, `JSON.stringify`, for the address; the notifications, the log entries and the email sink of the run hold none after the clearing; the sink holds only what was sent before, not the clearing's concern); idempotent (a second run, and two at once, clear once and answer 0 the second time); a run after days of downtime clears every row that ended longer ago, each once; what the row keeps (state, times, role, token hash) is unchanged but a `PENDING` row past the period, which is `EXPIRED`.
- Admission invitations: the same, for revoked (`revokedAt`) and expired; an accepted one keeps its address; the list answers without an address afterwards; a new invitation to the same address after the clearing works.
- Notifications: the notification of an invitation that expired is deleted by the timer, and a notification of a live invitation, of an accepted one, and of an offer or a removal is untouched; idempotent.
- The four are in `TIMERS`, run twice at once with the others without an error (`session-list.test.ts` lists them).
- Two runs that reach one row together: a test holds the row's lock while both runs read it as unmarked, so that both wait at the claim; one email results (a claim that ignores the mark fails it).
- End to end (Chromium), `tests/e2e/sharing.spec.ts`: the timer run twice 12 days in, the one email (subject and text), the reminder in the list with its date in the browser's zone and the link to the invitation, and the automated accessibility check of the list with it.

Mutations, each tried once on purpose and restored from a saved copy, named in the pull request: the reminder without its claim; the claim made after the email; the reminder for an unsent invitation; for a revoked one; for an expired one; one second early; an address left in the row; the period 29 days; the admission clearing counted from the creation; the clearing of an accepted invitation; the notification kept past an expiry; the title in the email.

Not tested: two instances of the whole application (D176); the browser's drawing of the reminder beyond one end-to-end case; the real email service (the local sink only).
